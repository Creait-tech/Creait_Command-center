## Primary Business Constraint

**Root:** the revenue engine is Dana — quoting, closing and commercial relationships run through one calendar, so sales speed and follow-through collapse to whatever her week allows.

**Shows up in:** Block 1 ("nobody else can price a job"), Block 2 (3–5 day quotes; loss reason is speed; 40% follow-up), Block 3 (four-week test fails on quoting), Block 6 (hand-off list starts with quoting). Four blocks; the rule requires two.

**Owner's Q8 belief:** "We need more leads. Marketing is the problem." **The evidence says:** ~85 leads a month already arrive; they leak at response, follow-up and quote speed. Leads are a symptom; the root is owner-bound sales capacity. Marketing work is real but sequenced after the bucket stops leaking.

**Contributors (max 3):** no follow-up system (P3) · static competitor pricing (P5) · manual integration that makes the office manager the glue (L2).

**Annual cost (three numbers, never merged):** ~$92K expected operating profit from slow quotes and missing follow-up · ~$28K staff payroll on automatable work · ~416 owner hours on quote assembly.
