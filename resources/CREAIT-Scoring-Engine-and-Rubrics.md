# CREAiT Score — Scoring Engine & Rubrics v1
### Advisor-only. The calculator in `04-Advisor-Calculator.html` implements this math.

## The model

- **30 scored indicators** — 10 per pillar. Advisor scores each 0–4 after the intensive (owner answers inform, advisor calibrates).
- **Scale (behaviorally anchored):** 0 Absent · 1 Informal · 2 Developing · 3 Established · 4 Scalable. **N/A** allowed — removed from the denominator, never counted as zero.
- **Evidence confidence** per indicator, tracked separately: **R** Reported (owner said so) · **D** Demonstrated (walked through it live) · **Doc** Documented (record/report seen). Confidence never changes the score — it widens financial ranges and gets flagged in the report.
- **Pillar score** = average of scored indicators ÷ 4 × 100.
- **CREAiT Score (0–100)** = Profit × 40% + Systems × 35% + Leverage × 25%.
- **Maturity bands:** 0–20 **Reactive** · 21–40 **Stabilizing** · 41–60 **Building** · 61–80 **Scaling** · 81–100 **Self-Running**.
- **Critical Constraint Overlay** (below) sits alongside the score — a warning can never be averaged away.
- Every report names **one Primary Business Constraint** (+ up to 3 contributors).

## PROFIT pillar (40%) — is the growth engine working?

| P8 | Growth ownership & measurement | Nobody owns marketing/sales; no numbers | Owners named; numbers reviewed sometimes | Accountable owners; leads/conversion/CAC/value reviewed together |
| P9 | Margin knowledge | Gross margin unknown | Known overall, not by job/service | Tracked by job/service/customer; decisions use it |
| P10 | Financial visibility | Tax-time-only financials | Monthly-ish, late, lightly used | Monthly within 15 days, reviewed, targets exist |

## Critical Constraint Overlay — check every engagement

Flag as an explicit warning (with trigger, evidence, business effect, resolution condition) regardless of scores:

- Largest customer > 25–30% of revenue
- Negative or perilously tight cash / debt distress
- Financial reporting too unreliable to base decisions on
- A key person or license whose loss stops the business
- Nontransferable or unwritten critical contracts
- Known legal / tax / insurance / compliance exposure → **refer to a professional**
- Owner burnout or health risk affecting continuity
- Sensitive data in uncontrolled tools / no backups

**Rule:** any active warning forces a "Prepare First" label on otherwise-attractive growth initiatives that depend on the weak foundation.

## Primary Business Constraint — selection discipline

The constraint must: materially limit the owner's stated objective · show up in multiple sections of evidence · explain recurring symptoms · be actionable. Present as **Symptoms → Root Constraint → Annual Cost → Required Capability → First Intervention → Measurement.** If two roots are inseparable, present the cluster honestly. Always compare against the owner's own Q8 belief — "you said X; the evidence says Y" (or "the evidence agrees with you") is the pivotal Results Session moment.

## Financial rules (calculator enforces)

- Every opportunity: **low / expected / high**, with cost, time-to-benefit, capacity check, confidence.
- Detailed cases for only the **2–3 strongest** profit opportunities; the rest go to the ranked backlog.
- **Overlap adjustment**: portfolio total = sum × overlap factor (default 0.7 unless justified) — never sum raw maximums.
- Automation savings = hours/wk × loaded rate × 52 × automation share. Report **capacity recovered separately from cash profit** — recovered hours are only dollars if they're redeployed or removed.
- **Evidence-to-range rule:** any opportunity resting mainly on Reported-only evidence gets its low/high bounds widened by ±25% and the report language "based on your estimates." Demonstrated/Documented evidence uses standard ranges.
- Never compound unrelated percentages into one headline number.
