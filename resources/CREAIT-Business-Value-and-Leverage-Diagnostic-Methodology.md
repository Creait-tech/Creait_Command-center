## 3. The Framework: one score, three pillars

Client-facing simplicity, Codex-grade mechanics underneath.

### Pillar 1 — PROFIT (the growth engine)
Profit-lever analysis organized as **More Leads / More Sales / More Transactions / More Profit**, gated by a **Foundations readiness check** (cash, people, capacity, measurement). Every deficiency dollarized; detailed low/expected/high financial cases for only the 2–3 strongest opportunities — overlap-adjusted, never a compounded fantasy headline.

### Pillar 2 — SYSTEMS (can this business run and grow without the owner?)
The exit-assessment DNA, repurposed as bottleneck discovery: owner dependence (decisions, sales, relationships, approvals), management depth, process documentation & adherence, accountability and meeting rhythm, key-person risk, customer concentration, recurring revenue, financial visibility. Includes the **Owner Absence Test** and **Owner-Required Decisions inventory** — the two most powerful Mirror instruments. Reported with a directional "what this means for your company's future value" narrative — no appraisal.

### Pillar 3 — LEVERAGE (what should AI and automation take off your plate?)
Workflow-first: inventory 10–15 core workflows (lead handling, quoting, scheduling, onboarding, delivery, QC, invoicing, collections, reporting…). For each: volume × time × people × loaded cost, error/rework rate, delay cost. Classify: eliminate / simplify / standardize / conventional automation / AI-assist / controlled AI agent / human-led. Output: a ranked **AI Opportunity Map** with annual dollar figures — *"Your team spends $31,200/yr on work AI handles for $200/mo."* This section is CREAIT's build pipeline.

### Scoring mechanics (under the hood)
- Behaviorally anchored 0–4 rubrics (Absent / Informal / Developing / Established / Scalable).
- Evidence confidence tracked separately (Reported / Demonstrated / Documented); low confidence widens ranges, never silently caps scores.
- "Not currently known" is an answer, never a zero.
- Section maximums auto-calculated from the active question set.
- **Critical Constraint Overlay** — severe risks surface as explicit warnings regardless of pillar scores.
- **Primary Business Constraint** — every report names ONE evidence-backed root constraint (+ up to 3 contributors): Symptoms → Root Constraint → Annual Cost → Required Capability → First Intervention → Measurement.

### The Three Futures section (kept, directional)
GROW / SCALE / EXIT modeled with their numbers — projected profit and owner-role outcomes per path, "what it demands of you" reality checks. Educates grow-vs-scale-vs-exit exactly as intended, without a formal appraisal. Every path's phase 2 runs through CREAIT.

## 4. The Psychological Journey: Mirror → Gap → Fork

| Stage | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **MIRROR** | Intake + 4-hr intensive | Owner hears themselves admit the bottleneck (Owner Absence Test, decisions inventory, "what would break at +20% sales?"). Working hypotheses tested openly, no premature verdict. |
| **GAP** | 90-min results session (day 7–10) | Score reveal → **the Cost of the Constraint**: profit opportunity left on the table (low/expected/high) + payroll burned on automatable work + owner hours trapped. Primary Business Constraint named with evidence. Three Futures shown. |
| **FORK** | Close of results session | Client picks three 90-day priorities — fully usable without CREAIT. Then the permission-based ask. The AI Workflow Blueprints in their hands ARE the proposals. Credit-forward expires day 60. |

**Client journey:** 20-min fit call (green/yellow/red; free 2-minute Snapshot screener as lead magnet/filter) → agreement + 50% → guided digital intake (adapt Codex Intake v1; 45–60 min, conditional, estimates OK, unknowns preserved; Pre-Assessment Summary back, never an auto-score) → 4-hour diagnostic intensive (on-site for $10K tier) → analysis window (Claude-powered pipeline) → 90-min results session (balance due within 48h after; package releases on payment) → 30-day live review → 90-day digital outcome check → 6-month re-score invitation. Weak businesses: reframe protocol — never demoralize; Profit is the hero pillar.

## 5. Deliverables

1. **Executive Blueprint** (~25–30 pp): one-page Value Realization Dashboard (constraint, five priorities, opportunity ranges — cash / revenue-enablement / capacity kept separate), score + pillars, Primary Business Constraint, Owner Dependence Profile, AI Opportunity Map, Three Futures, three-horizon roadmap (90 days / 12 months / 3-year destination), assumptions.
2. **Results presentation** — the reveal deck.
3. **Activation Toolkit** (editable): 90-Day Priority Plan, Initiative Tracker, KPI Scorecard, Weekly Leadership Meeting system, 3 full Activation Cards + 2 backlog cards, SOP Priority Map + template + one Model SOP, **3 AI Workflow Blueprints** (current vs. future workflow, human-control points, tool-independent requirements, date-stamped software shortlist, costs, payback, pilot plan), Financial Scenario Planner.
4. **Technical Appendix**: full scoring, evidence confidence, workflow inventory, opportunity register, methodology.

Perpetual internal-use license to configured deliverables; methodology and master assets remain CREAIT IP.

## 6. Ascension: two CREAIT lanes

- **Lane 1 — CREAIT Builds**: fixed-scope AI/automation implementations of the report's Workflow Blueprints (~$3–15K per build). Blueprint = pre-written proposal; scoping call is short because the diagnostic already did the work.
- **Lane 2 — CREAIT Advisory**: ongoing growth retainer (~$2,500–3,500/mo) — coaching + profit levers + systems installation, builds folded in or added, score re-tested quarterly, annual re-diagnostic included.
- Credit-forward applies to either lane. Typical path: one Lane-1 build (fast win, low commitment) → Lane-2 retainer.
- **Optional add-on**: formal business valuation, separately scoped through a qualified provider, for owners who want the number.
