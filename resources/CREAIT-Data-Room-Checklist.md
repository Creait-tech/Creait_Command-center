# CREAiT Growth & AI Diagnostic — Data Room Checklist v1
### Sent with the agreement and the intake link. Chased by the Intake Coordinator before the intensive. Approved 8 Sep 2026.

Owner-facing wording: *"Estimates get you a good diagnostic. Documents get you a defensible one. Anything you send is used only to verify what you tell us and is deleted 90 days after your results session unless you ask us to keep it."*

## Required for a full-confidence Profit pillar

If the P&L is missing, the report prints a disclosure that Profit findings rest on unaudited owner figures and widens **every** opportunity range by ±25%, because every dollar figure rests on owner-reported revenue and margin. Delivery is not blocked. The other four documents raise individual indicators from Reported to Documented.

| Document | Verifies | Indicators | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Profit & loss, last 24 months** (monthly if available) | Revenue, gross margin, operating profit, owner comp | P9, P10, margin-points headline | Accounting export, not a tax return. Mark `pnl_on_file` in the Command Center when received. |
| **Revenue by customer, trailing 12 months** | Concentration | S8, overlay | A report from the invoicing or accounting system. The single most-skipped document and the one that most often changes the constraint. |
| **Accounts receivable aging** | Cash pressure, collection lag | P10, overlay (cash) | As of the intake date. |
| **Rate card or price list, plus 3 recent quotes** | Pricing discipline, discounting | P5, P6 | Quotes should include one that was discounted. |
| **One job or project cost breakdown** | Margin by job/service | P9 | Any format. If none exists, that is itself the P9 evidence. |

## Collected in the intake, not as documents

Loaded labor rate per role (if payroll summary is not shared), owner hours per week, hours per repetitive task (Q44), largest-customer share estimate (Q14), P&L review cadence (Q20).

## Handling

- Store in the client's folder in the CREAiT Team Drive under 03 - INTAKE, CRM & ONBOARDING / Clients / *client name*. Never in email threads or chat.
- Record each received document in the Command Center review step (name, kind, date). The release gate reads `pnl_on_file` from there.
- Delete 90 days after the results session unless the client moves to Builds or Advisory.
