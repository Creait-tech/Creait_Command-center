# CREAIT Growth & AI Diagnostic — Master Intake Questionnaire v1
### Advisor source of truth. The client-facing version is `02-Intake-Form.html`.

**Design rules:** 45–60 min · estimates welcome · "Not currently known" is always a valid answer and is never scored as zero · uploads optional · no score shown to the client at completion · client receives a Pre-Assessment Summary only. Questions marked 🪞 are Mirror instruments — their job is to make the owner hear themselves name the bottleneck. Ask them exactly as written.

---

## Section 1 — You and the Business You Want (Mirror opening)

1. **Contact & company**: name, email, phone, company name, website, location, industry.
2. **Describe your company in plain language.** What do you sell, who do you serve, how is the work delivered? *(long text)*
3. **Primary objective for the next 1–3 years** *(pick 1 primary + up to 2 secondary)*: More profit & cash flow · Reliable revenue growth · Stronger sales/marketing engine · Better operations & delivery capacity · Reduce owner dependence · Use AI/automation effectively · Prepare to scale · Succession/leadership transition · Future sale or partial exit · Resilience/risk reduction · Other.
4. **What three measurable outcomes would make the next 12 months a success?** *(3 × outcome + target)*
5. **Describe the business you want to own 3 years from now** — size, profit, team, systems, and *your role in it*. *(long text)*
6. **Your current role** *(pick closest)*: Doing the technical work · Selling & managing customer relationships · Managing people & operations · Leading strategy & growth · Split across most of these.
7. 🪞 **Owner time & freedom**: hours/week now → desired in 12 months → desired in 3 years · longest uninterrupted time away in the past 12 months · *"What pulls you back in when you try to step away?"*
8. 🪞 **What do you believe is the single biggest bottleneck in the business right now?** What symptoms tell you that, and how long has it been true? *(long text — capture verbatim; this gets compared against the diagnostic finding in the results session)*

## Section 2 — Business Profile & Money

9. **Age & ownership**: year founded · owners & percentages · any owner misalignment affecting decisions?
10. **Team**: FT employees · PT · regular contractors · managers/team leads · people with direct reports.
11. **Reach**: local / regional / multi-state / national / online — primary markets.
12. **Revenue model** *(≈100% total)*: one-time projects % · recurring contracts % · repeat-without-contract % · products % · retainers % · other %.
13. **Top offerings** *(up to 5)*: name · % of revenue · rough gross margin if known · growing/flat/declining · strategic importance.
14. **Customer mix**: active customer count · B2B/B2C/mixed · % revenue from largest customer · % from top five · 🪞 *% of revenue tied directly to YOUR personal relationships*.
15. **Revenue**: last completed year · prior year · YTD · full-year forecast. *(ranges fine)*
16. **Two-year revenue trend**: declined >10% / declined ≤10% / flat / up ≤10% / up 11–25% / up >25% — and what caused it?
17. **Gross margin**: approximate % if known · main direct costs · is margin tracked by job/service/customer?
18. **Profit & owner pay**: operating profit estimate · owner salary · owner distributions · unusual one-off expenses. *(Used to size opportunities — this is not a valuation.)*
19. **Cash pressure** *(check all)*: comfortable · profitable-but-tight · customer payment timing · materials/inventory · payroll timing · debt payments · seasonality · big expense coming · cash not reviewed regularly.
20. **Financial visibility**: monthly-within-15-days / monthly-delayed / quarterly / tax-time-only / inconsistent / reports exist but unused — which reports actually get reviewed?
21. **Targets**: revenue · gross margin · operating profit · cash reserve · owner comp. *("No target" allowed — that's a finding.)*
22. **Optional uploads**: P&L (last 2–3 yrs), balance sheet, sales reports, KPI dashboard.

## Section 3 — The Growth Engine (Profit pillar)

23. **Lead sources** *(top 5)*: source · leads/month · cost if known · quality 1–5 · tracked?
24. 🪞 **Lead response**: what happens when an inquiry comes in? · typical response time · who is responsible · is every inquiry captured in one system? · automated follow-up? · % of leads that get 2+ follow-ups.
25. **Conversion**: lead→sale or proposal→close % · tracked by source/person/service? · defined sales process? · most common reason you lose.
26. **Transaction value**: typical first sale · typical annual customer value · most valuable segment · tracked?
27. **Repeat & retention**: % who buy again · renewal rate if applicable · are inactive customers identified? · reactivation campaigns ever run? · why customers leave.
28. **Pricing** *(check all true)*: reviewed annually · based on real job economics · varies by scope/value · discount rules defined · team can defend the price · unchanged 2+ years · competitor-based · owner-gut-based · pricing is a current worry. — When was the last real price change?
29. **Offers**: complementary services routinely offered? · bundles/packages? · premium option? · entry-level option? · customer needs you can't currently serve?
30. **Ownership of growth**: who owns marketing · who owns sales · monthly marketing budget · do sales & marketing review results together? · are leads, conversion, CAC, and customer value ever reviewed together?
31. 🪞 **Capacity test**: *"If sales jumped 20% in the next 90 days, what breaks first?"* — absorb comfortably / minor adjustments / quality slips / must hire / need equipment or capital / don't know.
32. **Owner's guess**: which 3 areas do you think hold the most near-term profit? *(list from the levers — compare with findings later)*

## Section 4 — Operations & Systems (Systems pillar)

33. **Critical workflows** *(up to 10)*: the workflows that generate revenue, deliver work, collect cash, keep quality (lead handling, quoting, scheduling, onboarding, delivery, QC, invoicing, collections, hiring…).
34. **Workflow ownership** *(matrix per workflow: always/usually/sometimes/rarely)*: one clear accountable role? · defined handoffs? · consistent exception escalation?
35. **Documentation**: % of critical recurring work that is documented · current · accessible · used in training · actually followed.
36. **Recurring failures** *(check all)*: missed handoffs · scheduling delays · incomplete info · complaints · quality inconsistency · rework · late billing · slow collections · unclear priorities · 🪞 *owner has to step in* · accountability problems. — Which one costs the most?
37. **Limiting resource**: owner time / leadership / skilled labor / sales capacity / delivery capacity / equipment / working capital / technology / process consistency / demand — and what's the evidence?
38. **Roles & accountability**: clear and followed / mostly clear with gaps / informal / 🪞 *everything falls back to the owner* / recurring accountability problems.
39. **Scoreboard**: how many KPIs reviewed weekly · does each have one owner · are targets visible · does a miss trigger action?
40. **Meeting rhythm**: weekly structured & action-oriented / weekly but loose / biweekly-monthly / reactive only / none — what happens to unresolved issues?

## Section 5 — Technology, AI & Automation (Leverage pillar)

41. **Core systems**: CRM · marketing · sales/proposals · scheduling/PM · delivery · accounting · reporting · communication · files/knowledge · HR/payroll. *(name each or "none")*
42. **Integration**: systems share data cleanly / some integrations / mostly separate + spreadsheets / same data entered multiple times / 🪞 *one person is the integration* — where does duplicate entry hurt most?
43. **Data trust**: KPI definitions agreed · data generally accurate · reports don't need manual cleanup · customer + financial data connectable · backups exist · we know where sensitive data lives. *(check what's true)*
44. 🪞 **Repetitive work inventory** *(top 5 tasks — the heart of the Leverage audit)*: task · who does it · frequency · minutes per occurrence · errors/rework? · systems used. *(Advisor computes: hours/yr × loaded rate = annual cost)*
45. **Current automation**: what's already automated (scheduling, reminders, sequences, proposals, invoicing, reporting…) and is it reliable?
46. **Current AI use**: drafting · research · marketing content · sales support · customer communication · estimating/proposals · knowledge retrieval · reporting · scheduling · none — which tools, who uses them, what result?
47. **AI/automation discipline** *(check what's consistently true)*: use cases picked by business impact · someone accountable per implementation · human-review rules clear · sensitive-data rules defined · results measured · failures detectable · process documented before automating · team trained.
48. **Tech & data risks** *(check all)*: shared passwords · unclear access · ex-employees may retain access · inconsistent backups · no tested recovery · sensitive data in unapproved tools · unclear account ownership · one-vendor/one-person dependence · none known.
49. **90-day readiness**: implementation budget range · owner hours/week available · team hours/week · appetite for changing systems.

## Section 6 — Owner Independence & Risk (Mirror finale)

50. 🪞 **What still requires YOU?** *(check all)*: pricing exceptions · major sales · customer escalations · hiring/firing · purchasing · scheduling · quality sign-off · banking/payments · vendor relationships · technical decisions · strategy. — *"Which three would you hand off first if you could?"*
51. 🪞 **The four-week test**: *"If you were unreachable for four weeks starting tomorrow — what keeps running, what slows, what stops, who decides, and what can nobody else access?"* *(structured long text — the single most revealing question in the intake)*
52. **Key-person & relationship risk**: any employee, customer, vendor, license, or knowledge whose loss would seriously hurt? *(risk table)*
53. **Contracts & continuity** *(check true)*: customer agreements written & accessible · vendor agreements written · contracts transferable · licenses/insurance current · employment agreements current · company owns its IP & digital accounts · continuity plan exists · no known legal/tax/insurance issue.
54. **Transferability priorities** *(pick 3)*: leadership depth · role clarity · financial reporting · sales without the owner · customer diversification · recurring revenue · SOPs/knowledge transfer · tech & data ownership · delivery consistency · contracts/compliance · succession.
55. **Implementation readiness**: ready with leadership capacity / ready if priorities stay tight / must resolve cash-staffing-leadership first / 🪞 *owner would have to drive everything* / uncertain. — What could stop the roadmap?

## Conditional Module E — Ownership Transition *(only if Q3 includes sale/succession/exit)*

E1. Transition type: family · management · employee buyout · outside sale · partial sale/investment · merger · gradual withdrawal · unknown.
E2. Horizon: <2 yrs · 2–3 · 4–5 · 6–10 · 10+ · unknown.
E3. Desired post-transition role. *(text)*
E4. What must be true, personally and financially, for it to feel successful? *(text — no valuation implied)*
E5. Advisors currently involved: CPA · tax · attorney · financial planner · insurance · broker/banker · valuation professional · none.
E6. What feels most uncertain about it? *(text)*
