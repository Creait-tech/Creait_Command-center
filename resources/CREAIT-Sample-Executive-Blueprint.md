# Part 4 — The Profit Opportunities

*Detailed cases for the three strongest. Every figure shows low / expected / high, and the portfolio is overlap-adjusted — these numbers are not additive.*

## Opportunity 1 — Speed-to-Quote + Follow-Up Engine
**Finding:** ~$1.7M of closed non-contract revenue comes from quotes that win ~35% of the time, and your most common loss is speed. **Evidence:** your loss-reason answer (R); 3–5 day turnaround (D, watched a quote being built); 40% follow-up rate (R). **Chain (calculator: close-rate lift):** $1.7M closed ÷ 35% = ~$4.86M quoted per year → lift of 2.5 / 5.0 / 8.0 points = $121K / $243K / $389K added revenue → × 38% gross margin = **$46K / $92K / $148K** operating profit annually → widened ±25% because close rate and quote volume are your estimates: **$35K / $92K / $185K, based on your estimates.** **Recommendation:** templated pricing + AI-drafted quotes + automated follow-up (Blueprints #1 and #2). **First action:** approve the pricing template structure. **Measurement:** turnaround hours · touches per quote · close rate. *(Setup ~$4–7K · benefit begins ~month 2 · confidence: Medium — the two inputs that matter most are Reported)*

## Opportunity 2 — Price Refresh + Job-Level Margin Tracking
**Finding:** prices static for three years amid rising costs; margin swings of 10+ points monthly, unexplained because jobs aren't tracked. **Evidence:** "last real change 2023"; monthly gross margin range 32–44%. **Chain (calculator: price change):** $1.7M non-contract revenue × +4% / +6% / +8% = $68K / $102K / $136K, flowing straight to contribution → minus the contribution on lost volume (4% / 3% / 2% of $1.7M × 38% = $26K / $19K / $13K) = **$42K / $83K / $123K** operating profit; margin tracking separately eliminates quietly unprofitable job types (unpriced here). Revenue and margin are Documented, so no widening. **Recommendation:** one price update with a value-framing script; margin fields added to every closed job. **First action:** approve the new rate card. **Measurement:** average job value · win rate (watch for over-loss) · gross margin by job type, monthly. *(Setup ~$2K · benefit ~month 1 · confidence: High on the arithmetic, Medium on the volume-loss assumption. This is your **Early Momentum Initiative** — a decision, not a project.)*

## Opportunity 3 — Reactivation + Residential Maintenance Plans
**Finding:** twelve years of customers in QuickBooks nobody contacts, and no recurring offer for residential. **Evidence:** "we never talk to them"; 45% residential repeat rate happening by accident. **Chain (calculator: reactivation):** ~900 dormant residential customers (QuickBooks export, Doc) × 5% / 8% / 12% reactivated = 45 / 72 / 108 customers × ~$1,800 average annual value (R) = $81K / $130K / $194K revenue → × 38% = **$31K / $49K / $74K** operating profit → widened ±25% on the Reported average value: **$23K / $49K / $92K, based on your estimates.** Maintenance-plan revenue is not priced here; every plan sold moves revenue from one-shot to recurring. **Recommendation:** quarterly reactivation sequence + a simple 2-tier maintenance plan. **First action:** export and segment the dormant list. **Measurement:** reactivated customers/month · plans sold · recurring revenue %. *(Setup ~$4K · benefit ~month 3 · confidence: Medium)*

## The three AI Workflow Blueprints
*(Each of these is scoped ready to build — by anyone you choose, including CREAiT.)*

### Blueprint #1 — Speed-to-Quote System
**Today:** site visit → notes → Dana builds each quote by hand in Sheets, 30–40 min, 3–5 days later. **Future:** crew lead captures photos + measurements in the field app → AI drafts the quote from your pricing template → you review and approve from your phone in ~3 minutes → sent same day, logged in one system. **Human control:** nothing sends without your (or later, ops manager's) approval; pricing rules are yours. **Requirements:** pricing template by job type; field-app quote module or connected quoting tool; AI drafting layer. **Software shortlist (July 2026 — reverify):** native quoting in your field-service platform + AI layer; alternatives quoted at build. **Cost:** ~$4–7K setup + $100–200/mo. **Payback:** ~2–3 months at expected case. **Risks & controls:** bad template = fast wrong quotes — we pilot on the top 5 job types first, 2-week shadow period, weekly accuracy check. **Pilot:** 20 quotes side-by-side vs. your manual pricing before it touches a customer.

### Blueprint #2 — Follow-Up & Reactivation Engine
**Today:** ~40% of quotes get a second touch; 12 years of dormant customers untouched. **Future:** every quote triggers day-2 / day-5 / day-10 touches (text + email, written in your voice, from your number) with replies routed to the office manager; dormant list gets a seasonal 3-touch reactivation sequence. **Human control:** sequences pause the moment a human replies; you approve every message template. **Cost:** ~$2–4K setup + $100–300/mo. **Payback:** first won quote pays for months. **Controls:** frequency caps, easy opt-out, no automation on active complaints. **Pilot:** 60 days on residential quotes only, measured against the prior 60.

### Blueprint #3 — Invoice & Collections Automation
**Today:** invoices wait 5–10 days on your approval, then get chased by hand; commercial pays in 45–60. **Future:** job completion triggers a drafted invoice → approval moves to ops manager (you see a daily summary) → automatic reminder ladder with payment links → escalation to a human at day 30. **Human control:** approval stays human — it just stops being *only you*; escalations are always a person. **Cost:** ~$2–3K setup + existing tools. **Payback:** cash arrives 5–10 days sooner + ~$6K/yr chasing time. **Controls:** Beacon and top-five accounts get a personal touch rule — no auto-reminders without office-manager review. **Pilot:** residential first, commercial after 30 clean days.

## Ground rules that protect you
Fix the process, then automate it · a human reviews anything customer-facing until proven · customer data stays in tools you control · nothing that makes hiring, firing, or credit decisions · every automation has an owner and a weekly check.
