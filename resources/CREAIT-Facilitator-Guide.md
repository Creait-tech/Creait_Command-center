# CREAiT Growth & AI Diagnostic — Facilitator Guide v1
### Maurice's delivery playbook. Voice: calm, confident, advisor — never salesy. The client should feel impressive, not impressed.

---

## 1. The Fit Call (20 min, free)

**Goal:** qualify honestly, set the frame, book the engagement. Not a sales pitch — a mutual fit check.

**Open (2 min):** "Tell me about the business — what you sell, who you serve, and what made you reach out now."

**Probe (10 min):** revenue ballpark · years operating · team size · what's the biggest frustration right now · what have you already tried · who else is involved in decisions · if we found the real constraint and a clear plan, do you have the capacity to act in the next 90 days?

**Qualification (internal, green/yellow/red):**
- 🟢 $500K–$5M revenue · 3+ yrs · engaged owner · real frustration · implementation capacity
- 🟡 Slightly outside range, or capacity unclear → proceed with eyes open, tighten scope
- 🔴 Decline/redirect: <$300K or pre-revenue · cash/legal/tax crisis (refer to the right professional) · wants guaranteed outcomes · wants tools without process change · owner won't participate · primarily needs a certified valuation → refer out

**Frame & close (5 min):**
> "Here's how it works. You'll complete a guided intake — 45 to 60 minutes, estimates are fine. Then we spend four focused hours together going through the business system by system. Then I do the analysis personally, and about a week later we sit down for 90 minutes and I show you three things: your CREAiT Score, the one constraint that's actually holding the business back, and exactly what it's costing you every year — plus the 90-day plan to start fixing it. Everything is yours to keep and act on, with or without us. It's $7,500 — half to schedule, and the balance only after you've sat through your results session and seen the findings. And if you decide to work with us on implementation within 60 days of that session, the full $7,500 is applied as credit, in full."

Send: agreement + invoice + intake link + **Data Room Checklist** (see `CREAIT-Data-Room-Checklist.md`). Prep note: "Estimates get you a good diagnostic; documents get you a defensible one. The P&L, revenue by customer and AR aging are the three that matter most." Without a P&L on file the report prints a disclosure and widens Profit ranges — tell them that plainly; most owners send it.

## 2. Before the Intensive

- Read the intake completely. Copy Q8 (their bottleneck belief) verbatim into the calculator.
- Pre-fill the calculator baseline. Note contradictions (e.g., "growth objective" + "no lead tracking").
- Mark 5–8 areas to press on. Draft 2–3 working hypotheses for the Primary Constraint. Hold them loosely.
- Confirm recording consent. Recording → transcript → feeds the report generator.

## 3. The Diagnostic Intensive (4 hours, with one 15-min break)

**Posture:** curious operator, not interrogator. Reflect patterns out loud as you go — "I'm noticing everything routes through you; let's test that" — but give no verdict today.

**Block 1 — Story & Destination (30 min).** Their journey, the 3-year picture, what "success in 12 months" means. Re-ask the bottleneck question live: *"Before we dig in — say it again in your own words: what do you believe is really holding this business back?"* (Write it down visibly.)

**Block 2 — The Growth Engine (60 min).** Walk the money path end to end: where a lead comes from → what happens in the first hour → follow-up → quote/proposal → close → price → delivery → invoice → payment → repeat purchase. At every step: *who does this, how long does it take, what happens when it goes wrong, how do you know?* Capture numbers for the calculator (leads, conversion, avg value, response time).
- 🪞 Key moments: "Walk me through the last lead you lost — what happened?" · "When did you last raise prices, and what happened?"

**Block 3 — Operations & the Machine (60 min).** The workflow inventory: for each critical workflow — owner? documented? followed? what breaks? Then the accountability layer: KPIs, meetings, who owns what.
- 🪞 Key moments: "Who besides you can close a sale / approve an invoice / calm an angry customer?" · **The four-week test, live:** "You're unreachable for four weeks starting tomorrow. Walk me through week one, day by day."

**Break (15 min).**

**Block 4 — Time, Tools & AI (45 min).** The repetitive-work inventory, task by task: who, how often, how long, in what tool. Ask each key person's week: "Where do the hours actually go?" Systems tour if on-site or via screen-share: watch them create a quote, an invoice, a report. Note every swivel-chair double-entry moment.

**Block 5 — Risk & Close (30 min).** Concentration, key people, contracts, cash pressure, data hygiene. Then close:
> "Here's what happens next. I've heard several patterns today — [name 2–3 openly, as questions, not verdicts]. I'll now score all thirty factors, run the numbers on every opportunity we've touched, and pressure-test what the real constraint is. We meet [date] for 90 minutes. I'll show you the whole picture — and the plan. Between now and then, you don't need to do anything."

**After:** same day, while fresh — score all 30 indicators in the Command Center against the v2 anchors (all five levels are written; use them), set evidence level and a note with the quote or document behind every score, fill the opportunity and automation tables, check overlay flags, draft the Primary Constraint. N/A only where the indicator cannot apply; "don't know" scores the lowest supportable anchor and is flagged Reported.

## 4. Analysis Window (days 2–8)

1. Finalize scores + evidence confidence.
2. Build the 2–3 detailed profit cases (low/expected/high, cost, time, capacity check) + ranked backlog.
3. Build the top-3 AI Workflow Blueprints (these are CREAiT Lane-1 proposals — scope them like you'd build them).
4. Pick the Primary Business Constraint. Test it: does it explain the symptoms across sections? Compare against their Q8 belief.
5. Pick the Early Momentum Initiative (measurable inside 30 days, low risk, in their control).
6. Generate the report in the Command Center. Run the **release gate** in the review step: every blocker must clear (25+ resolved, no thin pillar, evidence on every score, 10+ Demonstrated/Documented, notes everywhere, constraint + cost, ordered ranges with a finding, reviewer named). Acknowledge warnings (no P&L, concentration, potential below current). A second founder reads Appendix B and checks five numbers by hand. Cut anything you can't defend.
7. Mark delivered — this freezes the snapshot and locks the rows. Build the results deck from the delivered report. Send calendar confirmation + final invoice.

## 5. The Results Session (90 min) — Mirror → Gap → Fork

**1. Re-anchor (5 min).** "Twelve months from now you said success meant [their words]. Three years out, [their words]. Everything I'm about to show you is measured against that."

**2. The Mirror completes (10 min).** "When we started, you told me the biggest bottleneck was ___. Hold that thought."

**3. The Score (10 min).** CREAiT Score + band + three pillars. Explain calmly: "This isn't a grade. It's a map of where the machine is strong and where it leaks."

**4. The Constraint (15 min).** Reveal the Primary Business Constraint: symptoms → root → evidence. Then the comparison: *"You said X. The evidence says [X exactly / X is a symptom — the root is Y]."* Either way the owner feels seen, not judged.

**5. The Gap (15 min).** The annual cost, in three separate numbers — never merged: profit opportunity left on the table (low/expected/high, overlap-adjusted) · payroll burned on automatable work · owner hours trapped per year. Pause after this slide. Let it land.

**6. Three Futures (10 min).** Grow / Scale / Exit with their numbers. Ask: "Which of these is the business you described at the start?" They choose. You write it down.

**7. The Plan (15 min).** Five priorities → they pick three for the first 90 days (guide gently toward the right three). Each has an Activation Card: owner, first action, deadline, KPI, weekly rhythm. Include the Early Momentum Initiative. "This plan is complete. You can run it without us — everything you need is in the toolkit."

**8. The Fork (10 min).** Then, permission-based:
> "Would it be useful if I showed you where support would make the biggest difference?"
If yes — two lanes, briefly: **CREAiT Builds** (we build the top workflow blueprints — they're already scoped in your report) and **CREAiT Advisory** (we run the plan with you, monthly). "Your full $7,500 is applied as credit, in full, toward either lane if you start within 60 days of today's session. No decision needed today — let's book a 30-minute scoping call if you want to explore it."
Balance is due within 48 hours of this session; the full package releases on payment. **Non-payment protocol:** one friendly reminder at 48h, one call at day 5, package held meanwhile; day 10 unpaid → formal notice per the agreement. (Deposit already covers delivery cost — stay calm, stay kind, hold the document.)

## 6. Follow-through

- **Day 7:** revision window closes (one reasonable revision included).
- **Day 30:** 45-min progress review (included) — priorities status, KPI movement, barriers. Not consulting; accountability. Natural second Fork moment.
- **Day 60 (from the results session):** credit expires — one honest reminder a week before, no pressure theater.
- **Day 90:** 10-min digital outcome check → one-page planned-vs-actual snapshot.
- **Month 6:** free re-score invitation — "let's see what moved."
- Non-buyers: enroll in email Sequence 5 (see 12-Email-Campaigns); tag their weakest pillar for personalization.

## 7. Hard rules

Never withhold findings to force a sale · never inflate a number to justify the fee · never present recovered hours as cash profit · never automate a broken process — fix, then automate · anything legal/tax/HR/security → refer to a professional · the assessment must be worth $7,500 standing alone, every time.
