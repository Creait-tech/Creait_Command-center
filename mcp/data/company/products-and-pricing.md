# CREAIT OS — Products & Pricing

White-labeled GoHighLevel + AI automations, packaged into three monthly tiers. The product evolution:

> Content Catalyst (Nov 2025) → Creait OS (Jan 2026) → App Modernization + Credibility Suite (Feb–Mar 2026) → Lite Creait OS Offering

## Pricing Ladder

| Tier | Monthly | What it includes |
|---|---|---|
| **OS Lite — $297/mo** | $297 | "Answers your phone 24/7" + "Track every lead." Core GHL workspace, one AI Phone Rep number, basic missed-call text-back, single landing page, one automation. For solo operators and 1–5 person teams who need the front door covered. |
| **OS — $497/mo** | $497 | Everything in Lite + "Follow-ups that happen automatically." Multi-channel sequences (SMS / email / voicemail drop), 3 landing pages, lead scoring, calendar booking flow, weekly reporting. For service businesses doing $200K–$1M revenue. |
| **OS Pro — $797/mo** | $797 | Everything in OS + "It runs while you sleep." Full pipeline automation, custom AI agents, integrations with their existing stack (Google Workspace, accounting), monthly strategy call, priority support. For service businesses doing $1M–$5M revenue. |

## ICP

Small service-based businesses, 1–50 employees, $200K–$5M revenue.

## Setup

Every tier gets the Creait OS onboarding: account provision in the master agency, snapshot install, calendar + phone wiring, lead-source connections, two-week handover.

## Language rules (also see company-profile.md)

- Lead with the customer pain, not features
- "Answers your phone 24/7" not "AI Voice Agent"
- "Track every lead" not "Pipeline Management"
- Never say CRM, API, webhooks, funnel builder, smart automations
