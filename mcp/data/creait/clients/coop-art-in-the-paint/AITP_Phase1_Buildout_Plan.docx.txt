This document is the complete buildout plan for Phase 1 of the CREAiT OS installation for Art In The Paint. It covers every deliverable, the GoHighLevel configuration steps, copy and automation logic, and the exact order of execution from day one through launch.

Phase 1 has one goal: make Art In The Paint's digital presence work as hard as their real-world work. When a corporate sponsor, city official, or grant reviewer lands on the site, they should immediately understand what the organization does, trust it, and have a clear way to take the next step — automatically handled by the system.

WEEK 2 — LEAD CAPTURE FORMS & CRM PIPELINES


2A
Lead Capture Forms

Build 3 forms in GHL. Each form routes to its own pipeline stage and triggers its own automation sequence.

Form 1 — Corporate Sponsor / Partner Inquiry
Embed on /partner page. This is the highest-value lead form.

	•	First Name, Last Name (required)
	•	Organization / Company Name (required)
	•	Title / Role (required)
	•	Email (required)
	•	Phone (optional)
	•	How did you hear about Art In The Paint? (dropdown: Referral / Event / Instagram / Google / Other)
	•	What type of partnership are you interested in? (multi-select: Court Sponsorship / B.A.L.L. Program / GoodGain / Brand Activation / Other)
	•	Tell us about your organization and goals (text area)
	•	CTA Button: "Submit — Let's Build Together"

Form 2 — School / Community Organization Inquiry
Embed on /contact page and /programs page.

	•	First Name, Last Name (required)
	•	Organization Name (required)
	•	Your Role (required)
	•	Email (required)
	•	Phone (optional)
	•	Which program are you interested in? (dropdown: Heritage Courts / B.A.L.L. Program / Policy at Play / GoodGain / General Inquiry)
	•	Tell us about your community or school (text area)
	•	CTA Button: "Submit — Let's Connect"

Form 3 — Donor / Supporter Lead

3B
AI Chat Agent — CREAiT OS Bot

Configure the AI chat widget in GHL (Bot/Conversations AI). Train it using AITP's onboarding form content, FAQ questions, and program descriptions.

Bot Persona
Bot Name
Courtside — Powered by Art In The Paint
Tone
Warm, confident, community-first. No jargon. Speaks like a knowledgeable community member.
Greeting
"Hey! I'm Courtside. I can tell you about our programs, how to partner with us, or what it takes to transform a space in your community. What do you need?"

Bot Training — FAQ Topics to Cover
	•	How much does it cost to transform a space?
	•	What kind of impact can we expect from a partnership?
	•	How long does a project take from start to finish?
	•	What is our role as a corporate sponsor?
	•	How do you keep the space active long-term?
	•	What is GoodGain and how does it work?
	•	How do we get started?
	•	Can we partner if we're outside Atlanta?

Bot Escalation Rules
	•	If visitor asks about pricing → Collect name + email → Route to Sponsorship pipeline (New Inquiry)
	•	If visitor is a school or community org → Route to Community Partner pipeline
	•	If visitor wants to donate → Link to /donate page
	•	If visitor mentions "media" or "press" → Collect info and tag as Media Inquiry
	•	All collected contacts → Automatically added to CRM with tag: "Chat Lead"


WEEK 4 — REPUTATION WORKFLOW & REPORTING DASHBOARD


4A
Reputation Workflow

Trigger: Contact moves to "Active Partner" or "Active Sponsor" stage in CRM. The system sends an automated request asking for a Google Review or testimonial.

Workflow Steps
	•	Contact reaches Active stage in pipeline
	•	Wait 3 days (give them time to experience the partnership)
	•	Send SMS: "[First Name], working with your team has been meaningful work. If you have 60 seconds, we'd love a Google review. [link]"
	•	If no action in 5 days → Send follow-up email with same request and a testimonial form option
