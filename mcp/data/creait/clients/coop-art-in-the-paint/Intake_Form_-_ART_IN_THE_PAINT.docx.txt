4. One-Line Description
Art In The Paint transforms public spaces into vibrant community hubs through art, sports, and technology while building a youth-powered rewards economy through GoodGain.

5. Full Description
Art In The Paint is a community development organization that uses art, athletics, and technology to improve public safety, increase community engagement, and enhance quality of life.
Through its People, Places, Policy framework, the organization has transformed 50+ courts and activated over 70 large-scale public art and sports spaces across Atlanta and globally. These spaces become platforms for youth development, cultural expression, and community connection.
Art In The Paint also develops GoodGain, a fintech-enabled platform that rewards young people for positive behaviors such as education, civic engagement, and participation in sports and arts. By combining physical infrastructure with digital incentives, the organization creates a scalable ecosystem that drives long-term behavior change and economic opportunity.

6. Problem
Communities—especially underserved ones—face three connected challenges:
	•	Underinvested and unsafe public spaces
	•	Limited access to structured youth development opportunities
	•	Lack of scalable systems to reward positive behavior and economic participation
Traditional programming is often temporary, underfunded, and not designed to scale or sustain engagement.

7. Solution
Art In The Paint builds infrastructure, not programs by integrating:
	•	Physical Transformation (Places): Revitalizing courts and parks into safe, beautiful, multi-use community hubs
	•	Community Activation (People): Programming sports, arts, and leadership experiences
	•	Systems Change (Policy): Convening stakeholders to improve public safety and equity
	•	Digital Layer (GoodGain): A fintech rewards platform that incentivizes positive youth behavior
This creates a continuous loop of engagement, ownership, and measurable impact.
