OVERVIEW & GUIDING PRINCIPLES

This document is the complete implementation brief for the Quantum Wealth Network (QWN) CRM build in Go High Level. It is organized into five Mini Projects, each representing a distinct system to be built and activated. The CREATE team should treat each Mini Project as a self-contained build module with clear inputs, outputs, and advancement logic.

THE LANGUAGE OF THE MISSION
QWN is not a service firm — it is a movement. Every touchpoint, pipeline stage, email, and automation inside GHL must reflect that. The people we serve are not simply 'clients.' They are Founders and Members. Founders are the business clients of Quantum Wealth Strategies (the Financial Services arm of QWN) who have an active bookkeeping and financial services relationship. Members are participants in the broader Quantum Wealth Network community. Every Founder is also a Member. Language throughout the build should always honor the journey: they are Captains of their financial journey, and this platform is Mission Control.

The QWN Universe Theme
All language, naming, and tone inside the GHL system should echo the Quantum Wealth Adventure framework. Core metaphors to embed throughout:
	•	Rocket Ship = the Founder's or Member's financial vehicle — their business, their wealth journey
	•	Fuel = Financial health and smart decision-making
	•	Hangar (Planet Zero) = Where every Founder begins — intake, diagnostic, discovery
	•	Mission Control = The QWN platform and support team
	•	Q-Pilot = The AI avatar and automation engine that guides Founders and Members through every stage. Q-Pilot is both a chatbot and a voice agent, and it is the unified persona for all automated touchpoints, reminders, nudges, and AI-assisted guidance inside the platform.
	•	The Three Galaxies = The progression of the wealth journey: THEORY (Galaxy 1), STRATEGY (Galaxy 2), NETWORK (Galaxy 3)
	•	North Star = The ultimate legacy milestone — the end of the 5-Year Journey

MINI PROJECT 1  LEAD NURTURING & PIPELINE BUILD
🚀  Clearing the Hangar. Getting every Founder launch-ready.

Purpose
Build a clean, structured pipeline in GHL that moves warm leads from first contact to Active Founder status. This includes a one-time contact cleanup and a fully automated nurture sequence that uses Q-Pilot as the touchpoint voice.

1A. Contact Database Cleanup
The existing contact database will be exported from the previous GHL account and imported into the new instance. The CREATE team is responsible for this cleanup using available automation/enrichment tools.

Cleanup tasks:
	•	Import exported contacts into new GHL instance
	•	Use GHL enrichment or connected tools to fill in missing data fields: email, phone, business name, service type, lead source (where detectable)
	•	Add notes field for any context that cannot be auto-populated
	•	Apply appropriate pipeline stage tag (see Stage Definitions below) based on current relationship status
	•	Remove duplicates and archive any clearly dead contacts

MINI PROJECT 2  FOUNDER ONBOARDING PROCESS
🚀  Fueling the rocket before it leaves the hangar.

Purpose
Once a lead signs their Engagement Letter (Stage 1), they begin the formal onboarding process. Onboarding runs through Stages 1–3 and is complete when all documents are received, QuickBooks is set up, and the 4-part educational series is finished. Only then does a Founder become Active (Stage 4). During this educational 4-part series, we'll also be working on any initial clean-ups that we'll need to do as they transition into the Active client stage.

2A. Onboarding Flow Overview
The onboarding process has two phases before the educational series:

Phase 1 — Stage 1: Contract & Agreement
	•	Trigger: Discovery call completed, SOW scoped, Engagement Letter sent
	•	GHL automation sends Engagement Letter via DocuSign or equivalent
	•	Q-Pilot follows up via email + SMS if unsigned after 3 days
	•	On signature: contact auto-advances to Stage 2 + triggers document collection sequence

Phase 2 — Stage 2: Document Collection & QB Setup
Stage 2 is a gate. The Founder cannot advance to Stage 3 until ALL required items below are confirmed received:
	•	Signed Engagement Letter / Contract (Stage 1 exit criterion, confirmed again)
	•	Business entity documents (LLC/Corp/etc.)
	•	Bank statements (as required by scope)
	•	QuickBooks Online access granted to QWS team
	•	Tax returns or prior financials (if cleanup is in scope)

GHL BUILD NOTE
Build a Stage 2 checklist inside the contact record or as a GHL task list. Each item should be checkable. Q-Pilot sends automated reminders for any outstanding item every 3–4 days until all boxes are checked. Once all items are marked complete, the system auto-tags the contact as 'Ready for Orientation' and notifies the internal team to advance them to Stage 3.

Phase 3 — Stage 3: The 4-Part Educational Series
The educational series is currently delivered live by John and will transition to a self-paced pre-recorded course hosted inside the QWN community. For now, the GHL pipeline should support both delivery modes.

The 4 modules are:
	•	Orientation: Welcome to Quantum Wealth Network. How the community and club works. The big picture of what being a Founder means. Introduction to Q-Pilot and Mission Control.
	•	Business Foundations 101: Core concepts every business owner needs — entity structure, separation of personal and business finances, foundational financial literacy.
	•	Profit First Setup: Introduction to the Profit First methodology. How QWS implements it. What the Founder needs to understand and do on their end.
	•	Fractional CFO Strategy: The difference between accounting and finance. What the next level of service looks like. Painting the picture of where the Founder is going.

GHL should track completion of each module. Q-Pilot sends a congratulatory message after each module completion and a prompt to schedule the next session or access the next module.

STAGE 4 ACTIVATION
When all 4 modules are marked complete, the contact auto-advances to Stage 4 (Active Founder). A welcome-to-the-crew message is sent from Q-Pilot: 'You've cleared the hangar. Your rocket is airworthy. Welcome to the crew, [First Name].' This moment should feel like a celebration.

Purpose
The QWN Community is the living world inside the platform. It is where Founders and Members grow, learn, connect, and progress along their wealth journey. It contains four primary components.

3A. The Mini Jumpstart Series
A free or low-barrier mini-course designed to introduce the QWN methodology and attract Members before they become Founders.

Build requirements:

	•	Short-form, accessible content — designed to be completed in one sitting or across a few days

A curated resource library available to all Members and Founders. Think Mission Control's reading room — the intelligence hub.

Content categories:

Build requirements:

MINI PROJECT 4  PARTNER PORTAL
🚀  Recruiting the crew. Building the fleet of aligned professionals.

Purpose
The Partner Portal is the intake, management, and engagement hub for financial professionals and aligned service providers who want to be part of the Quantum Wealth Network ecosystem. Partners serve as crew members in the QWN universe — specialists who support Captains on their journey.

4A. Partner Application Flow
Entry into the partner network begins with the formal application process:
	•	Prospective partner discovers QWN (via John, events, social media, referral, or website)
	•	They complete the QWN Partner Application (full survey — already built, see Intake Surveys document)
	•	Application is submitted and triggers an internal notification to the review team (John / ACE / Monica)
	•	Partner Evaluation Rubric scoring is completed by the review team (5 categories, 25 points max)
	•	Decision is logged and partner is moved to the appropriate pipeline stage

Q-PILOT — MISSION CONTROL AI
The voice, guide, and automation engine of the Quantum Wealth Network

Who Q-Pilot Is
Q-Pilot is the AI avatar and automation persona for the entire QWN platform. Every automated touchpoint — every email, text, chatbot response, and voice interaction — is delivered as Q-Pilot. Q-Pilot is not a tool. It is a crew member. It speaks with authority, warmth, and clarity. It never condescends. It never overwhelms. It guides.

Q-Pilot is:
	•	A chatbot inside the GHL community platform — answering FAQs, guiding navigation, supporting Founders and Members
	•	A voice agent — for call flows, appointment reminders, or audio touchpoints
	•	The delivery voice for all automated sequences: nurture, onboarding, 90-day QWT journey, partner onboarding, milestone celebrations, re-engagement
	•	The dashboard co-pilot — interpreting financial inputs and surfacing next steps

Q-Pilot Tone Guidelines
	•	Speaks directly and confidently — no filler, no fluff
	•	Anchored in the space/journey metaphor — uses the language of captains, rockets, fuel, galaxies naturally but not forcefully
	•	Warm and human — feels like a trusted advisor, not a chatbot
	•	Always forward-focused — every message moves the Captain toward the next milestone
	•	Celebrates wins, acknowledges challenges, never shames or lectures

FOR THE CREATE TEAM
Q-Pilot will have a custom avatar (visual design TBD — to be provided by John). When building all automation sequences, name the sender 'Q-Pilot' and write copy in Q-Pilot's voice. All emails should carry the Q-Pilot signature. SMS messages should open with 'Q-Pilot here —' or similar. The avatar will be attached once the visual brand guide is shared.
