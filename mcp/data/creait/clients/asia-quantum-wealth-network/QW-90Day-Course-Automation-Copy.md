## SEQUENCE OVERVIEW

**Goal**: Drive course completion over 90 days with a drip sequence of email + SMS touchpoints. Maintain engagement without overwhelming. Celebrate wins, acknowledge challenges, reconnect to purpose.

**Trigger**: Member or Founder enrolls in the full Quantum Wealth Theory (QWT) course.

**Audience**: Members and Founders who have begun the QWT curriculum — the flagship educational journey through the three galaxies (Theory, Strategy, Network).

| Day | Channel | Intent |
