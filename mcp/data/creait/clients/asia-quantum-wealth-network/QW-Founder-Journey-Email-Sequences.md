## FAQ Responses (For Q-Pilot Chatbot / Email Auto-Replies)

**Q: "How much does this cost and what exactly am I paying for?"**
A: Every project is different. Cleanup pricing ranges from $1,500 to $10,000 depending on how many months need reconciling, the number of accounts, and the complexity of your books. Monthly maintenance starts at a flat rate once your books are clean. Fill out the intake form and we'll send you an exact quote within 24 hours.

**Q: "How long will this take and what do you need from me?"**
A: Most cleanups take 2-4 weeks once we have all your documents. We need QuickBooks access, bank statements for the cleanup period, and your business entity docs. The more organized your docs are upfront, the faster we move.

**Q: "Will my books being cleaned up actually help me get funding or a loan?"**
A: Absolutely. Lenders need clean financials to approve you. A proper P&L, balance sheet, and reconciled books show you're serious and creditworthy. We've had founders get approved for funding they were previously denied — just by getting their books right.

**Q: "What happens at tax time, do you handle that too?"**
A: We prepare your books so your CPA or tax preparer has everything they need. Clean books = clean tax filing. We don't file taxes directly, but we work closely with tax professionals in our partner network to make sure the handoff is seamless.

**Q: "I've never had a CFO or bookkeeper before, where do I even start?"**
A: Start with the intake form. We'll review your situation and recommend the right service level. For most founders, the journey starts with a cleanup — getting your books current and accurate. From there, we set you up on monthly maintenance so you never fall behind again.
