BIGGER THAN A TITLE:
The Self-Trust Assessment for People Who Were Made for More
Discover the exact pattern that's been keeping you from the life you're capable of.

THE QUESTIONS & SCORING
1. When you imagine doing the thing you really want to do, what stops you first?
	•	A) I worry what people will think → Pretender
	•	B) I tell myself I'm not ready yet → Performer
	•	C) I don't have the support or resources around me → Prisoner
	•	D) I don't actually believe I deserve it → Provider
2. How do you typically show up for others vs. yourself?
	•	A) I give everyone else my best and save my leftovers for me → Provider
	•	B) I show up great publicly but fall apart privately → Pretender
	•	C) I'm ready for everyone else's dreams but stuck on my own → Prisoner
	•	D) I perform confidence but feel like a fraud inside → Pretender
3. When was the last time you fully trusted yourself?
	•	A) Honestly? I'm not sure I ever have → Prisoner
	•	B) I used to, but something happened → Provider
	•	C) I trust myself for others, never for me → Provider
	•	D) I trust my skills but not my worth → Performer
4. When opportunity shows up, what's your first instinct?
	•	A) I shrink back and let it pass → Prisoner
	•	B) I say yes but secretly hope it falls through → Pretender
	•	C) I look around to see if anyone else thinks I deserve it → Performer
	•	D) I go for it but spend the whole time waiting to be exposed → Pretender
5. What does your inner voice sound like most days?
	•	A) A strict parent who needs everything perfect before moving → Performer
	•	B) A martyr who reminds me how much I've sacrificed for others → Provider
	•	C) A prisoner who says "people like me don't get to do that" → Prisoner
	•	D) An imposter who says "they're going to find out you're faking" → Pretender
6. When someone gives you a genuine compliment, you:
	•	A) Deflect it immediately and redirect to someone else → Provider
	•	B) Accept it graciously but privately don't believe it → Pretender
	•	C) Feel guilty like you don't deserve it yet → Performer
	•	D) Wonder what they want from you → Prisoner
7. Your dream is sitting right in front of you. What's the most honest reason you haven't grabbed it?
	•	A) I'm waiting until I'm more prepared → Performer
	•	B) I'm waiting until my family is taken care of first → Provider
	•	C) I'm waiting for someone with authority to tell me I'm ready → Prisoner
	•	D) I'm waiting to feel like I actually belong there → Pretender
8. What would trusting yourself fully actually cost you?
	•	A) The version of me everyone depends on → Provider
	•	B) My excuse for why I haven't done it yet → Performer
	•	C) The comfort of staying invisible → Prisoner
	•	D) The story I've been telling myself about why I can't → Pretender
