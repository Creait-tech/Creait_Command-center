Welcome to Your Automation Engine
Rhavynn, this guide walks you through the 13 automation workflows that have been built and published inside your CREAIT OS sub-account. These systems are designed to run in the background so you can focus on coaching, creating, and connecting with your audience.
Every workflow is live and ready to go. This document explains what each one does, when it fires, and what you can expect to happen automatically.

How Your Automations Work
Your automations are organized into three categories: lead nurture sequences, operational workflows, and internal notifications. They all live inside the RAD Media Automations folder in your Automation tab.
Triggers are the events that start a workflow (someone fills out a form, makes a purchase, books a session, etc.). Actions are what happens next (emails get sent, tags get applied, pipeline opportunities get created, and you get notified).

Detailed Workflow Breakdowns
WF1: Newsletter Welcome
Purpose: When someone subscribes to your newsletter, this workflow instantly welcomes them and tags them for future segmentation.
Trigger: Newsletter form submission
What happens:
	•	Contact is tagged as newsletter-subscriber
	•	A branded welcome email is sent from Rhavynn Drummer (info@radmediastudios.com)
	•	Contact enters your lead nurture pipeline

WF2: Quiz Completed — Archetype Router
Purpose: This is the brain of your archetype system. When someone completes your assessment quiz, this workflow reads which archetype tag they received and routes them to the correct email nurture sequence.
Trigger: Quiz/assessment completed
Routing logic:
	•	archetype-performer tag → routes to WF3
	•	archetype-provider tag → routes to WF4
	•	archetype-prisoner tag → routes to WF5
	•	archetype-pretender tag → routes to WF6

WF7: Workshop Registration
Purpose: When someone registers for a workshop, this workflow confirms their registration, tags them appropriately, and creates a trackable opportunity in your Workshop Registration pipeline.
What happens:
	•	Contact is tagged with offering-workshop and pipeline-workshop
	•	An opportunity is created in the Workshop Registration pipeline
	•	A branded confirmation email is sent with workshop details

WF8: Workshop Reminders
Purpose: Automated reminder emails are sent leading up to the workshop date to reduce no-shows and keep registrants engaged and excited.

WF9: 1:1 Booking Confirmation
Purpose: When a client books a 1:1 coaching session, this workflow sends them a branded confirmation email so they know their session is locked in.

WF10: Project Submission
Purpose: When someone submits a project through your intake form, this workflow handles everything automatically.
What happens:
	•	Contact is tagged with project-submission and pipeline-project
	•	An opportunity is created in the Project Submissions pipeline (Submitted stage)
	•	A branded auto-reply email confirms their submission was received

WF11: Support Intake
Purpose: When a client or prospect submits a support request, this workflow creates a trackable support ticket, notifies you immediately, and sends the contact a confirmation that their request was received.
What happens:
	•	Contact is tagged with support-request and pipeline-support
	•	An opportunity is created in the Support pipeline (New stage)
	•	A branded auto-reply email is sent to the contact
	•	You receive an internal notification email with the contact details

WF12: Daily Support Digest
Purpose: Every morning, this workflow sends you an internal email reminding you to check your Support pipeline for any open tickets that need attention. This ensures nothing falls through the cracks.

WF13: Purchase Confirmation
Purpose: When a customer makes a purchase through your store, this workflow handles the full post-purchase experience.
What happens:
	•	Contact is tagged with pipeline-coaching
	•	An opportunity is created in the 1:1 Coaching pipeline (Booked stage, status: Won)
	•	A branded purchase confirmation email is sent to the customer
	•	You receive an internal notification email with the buyer details

Next Steps
Your automation engine is fully built and live. Here are a few things to keep in mind:

	•	Test your forms. Submit a test entry through each form (newsletter, quiz, workshop, project, support) to verify the full workflow fires correctly.
	•	Check your pipelines. After testing, visit Opportunities to confirm that pipeline cards are being created in the right stages.
	•	Review your emails. Check the test emails in your inbox to make sure the branding, copy, and links all look right.
	•	Set your custom values. Make sure your checkout links are set in Settings > Custom Values (linksfreedomcheckout and linksunlockcheckout).
	•	Drive traffic. Once everything checks out, start sending people to your assessment quiz and newsletter signup. The system handles the rest.
