Sequence Architecture
How it works: A contact takes the Self-Trust Assessment (8 questions). Their answers are scored to determine their dominant archetype. They immediately receive a Result Email with their archetype diagnosis. Then over the next 8 days, they receive a 3-email nurture sequence tailored to their archetype — each building toward the right offer.
