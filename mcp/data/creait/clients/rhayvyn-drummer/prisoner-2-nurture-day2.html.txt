Hey {{contact.first_name}},
I want you to think about something today.
Somewhere along the way, somebody — or something — told you that what you wanted wasn't for you. Maybe it was a teacher who didn't see your potential. Maybe it was a family member who thought they were protecting you. Maybe it was a system that was never built with you in mind.
And you believed it. Not because you're weak — but because when you hear something enough times, especially from people you trust, it starts to sound like truth.
But it was never truth. It was just a story someone else wrote for you.
The Prisoner archetype isn't about lacking talent. It's about lacking the belief that your talent is allowed to take up space.
Today I want you to do one thing: identify the voice. Whose voice is it that tells you "people like me don't get to do that"? Is it yours — or is it borrowed?
Because once you realize the voice doesn't belong to you, you can start deciding which voices actually get to stay.
