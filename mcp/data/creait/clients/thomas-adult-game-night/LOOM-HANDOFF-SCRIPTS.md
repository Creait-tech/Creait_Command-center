## VIDEO 2 — Reviewing AI-Drafted Replies (3 min)

**Loom URL:** `_____________`

### Open with:
"Right now your AI Sales Rep is in Suggestive mode. That means it writes the reply, but you have to hit Send. Here's how to review fast."

### Walk through:

1. **Conversations tab** → click any conversation thread (DM, SMS, or Live Chat)
2. Show the AI-suggested reply at the bottom (purple banner: "AI suggests...")
3. **Three options:**
   - ✅ Looks good → click **Send**
   - ✏️ Almost — click **Edit** → tweak → Send
   - ❌ Way off — click **Discard** → write your own
4. **Critical:** if AI suggests anything that doesn't sound like you (too formal, marketing-speak, fake hype), HIT DISCARD. Don't send corporate copy in your name.
5. **After 7-14 days of this:** if 80%+ of AI suggestions are clean, you can flip the AI to **Auto-Pilot mode** (Settings → AI Agents → Adult Game Nights Sales Rep → Auto Reply: ON). This means AI sends without asking. Only do this once you trust it.
6. **What to escalate to yourself anyway:**
   - Sponsor inquiries (anything mentioning brand/business/partnership)
   - Service bookings (anything about "game night for X people on Y date")
   - Negative feedback (any complaint or refund request)
   - Press / media (any mention of features/interviews)

### Close with:
"Bottom line: trust but verify. AI doesn't replace you on the high-touch stuff. It handles the volume so you can focus on the deals."

## VIDEO 3 — Handling Escalations (3 min)

**Loom URL:** `_____________`

### Open with:
"When a contact gets the `escalate-to-thomas` tag, that's the system telling you something needs your personal touch. Here's the playbook."

### Walk through:

1. **How escalations happen:**
   - AI agent detects: refund request / negative review / "I'm pissed" / "talk to a real person" → adds `escalate-to-thomas` tag → triggers internal SMS to your phone
   - Sponsor inquiries (form submission) → SMS notification
   - Service bookings (form submission) → SMS notification
2. **When you get the SMS notification:** open the Conversations tab on phone or desktop → search by contact name or scroll your inbox
3. **Response framework:**
   - Read every message in the thread (don't reply blind to the latest one)
   - If it's anger or frustration: acknowledge first, no defensiveness — "Yo {{name}} — heard you, that's not what we want. Let me make it right."
   - If it's a question AI couldn't answer: just answer it directly in your voice
   - If it's a high-value sponsor or service inquiry: don't email back — call them. People who fill out sponsor forms convert 4x faster on a phone call than email
4. **After you handle it:** remove the `escalate-to-thomas` tag → add `escalated-resolved` so the system knows it's done
5. **If you can't respond within 24h:** send a short "Yo — saw your message, I'm pulling up tonight with a real answer" so they don't ghost

### Close with:
"You don't have to do everything. But escalations are where deals are won or lost. That's the work."

## VIDEO 4 — Checking Pipeline Health (2 min)

**Loom URL:** `_____________`

### Open with:
"This is how you see how much money is in the pipeline at any moment — and which deals are about to die."

### Walk through:

1. **Opportunities tab** → top filter: **All Pipelines** dropdown → show 3 pipelines:
   - **Game Sales (E-com)** — Shopify auto-pipes here
   - **Game Night Service** — service bookings land here
   - **Sponsorship & B2B** — sponsor inquiries land here (once built)
2. Click into **Game Night Service** → show stages: Inquiry → Deposit Paid → Confirmed → Completed
3. **What to look for:**
   - Deals sitting in `Inquiry` > 72 hours = need follow-up text now
   - Deals in `Deposit Paid` for > 14 days without `Confirmed` = something is broken, call them
   - Deals in `Confirmed` past their event date = need to manually move to `Completed` (or set up a workflow for it)
4. **Total dollar value:** at top of each pipeline you'll see total $ across all open opportunities. That number is your forecast.
5. **Critical:** every Monday morning, spend 10 min in this view. It tells you exactly where the gaps are.

### Close with:
"Pipeline view is the difference between guessing and knowing. Look at it every Monday — that's the deal."

## VIDEO 5 — Manually Tagging a Contact (1 min)

**Loom URL:** `_____________`

### Open with:
"Tags are how the system knows what to do with each contact. Most are automatic. Some you'll add by hand. Here's how."

### Walk through:

1. **Contacts tab** → search by name or email → click contact
2. Right side panel: **Tags** section
3. Click the **+** next to Tags → type the tag name → select or create
4. **Common tags you'll add by hand:**
   - `vip` — contact you want extra attention on
   - `signed-sponsor` — sponsor deal closed
   - `sponsor-engaged` — sponsor responded to your pitch (stops the auto-follow-up sequence)
   - `reviewed` — contact has left a review (stops review-request sequence)
   - `escalate-to-thomas` — force this contact into your manual queue
   - `do-not-contact` — they asked to stop hearing from you
5. **Remove a tag:** hover the tag → click the X
6. **Bulk tag:** Contacts list → check multiple contacts → Actions → Add Tag

### Close with:
"Tags are how you control the system. If something's behaving weird with a contact, 9 times out of 10 it's because the wrong tag is on or the right tag is missing. Check the tags first."
