## Email_Surety_03_Credit_Explained (Day 3)

**Subject:** How bond pricing actually works (credit explained)

```
{{contact.first_name}},

A surety bond is NOT insurance on you — it's insurance on your obligation. The surety (bond issuer) is betting you'll perform. Credit is how they measure that bet.

Typical pricing for a $50K Medicare bond:
  · 720+ credit: ~$500–$750/year
  · 680–719: ~$750–$1,200
  · 640–679: ~$1,200–$2,500
  · <640: specialty markets, higher rates, still obtainable

If your credit isn't where you want it, I have markets that look at business cash flow instead. Reply and I'll walk through options.

— Sabrina
```
