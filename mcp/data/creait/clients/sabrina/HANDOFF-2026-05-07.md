## Honest summary

The clean wins of this session — code commits, JSON specs, provisioner runs, pipelines, branding, tags, bot scaffold — are real progress and represent ~60% of Phase 1 by surface area. The remaining 40% is what Chrome DevTools couldn't punch through cleanly: drag-and-drop form building, deeply nested workflow editors, Bot Goals/Knowledge Base tabs, calendar detail editor.

Those remaining UI tasks are routine clicking work for a human in CreaitOS — slow but not hard. The agentic approach hit its limit at iframe-sandboxed builders. Future-you (or whoever picks this up) should not waste hours trying to automate them; just open CreaitOS and click.

The bigger blocker for Phase 1 actually shipping is the deferred items: **Railway deploy + DNS + A2P registration**. Without those, no form submit goes anywhere and no nurture email lands in an inbox. Those are quick to do once you stop deferring them.
